    三个方案Three fronts, each from one benchmark

    Each direction takes one concrete device from a piece of award-winning or canonical work. All three show the full mark (rings or solid dots, switch above) and set 西顶文化 directly under 西頂廣仁宮. The back stays as it is.

    G · 点阵为格 Grid

    From Kazunari Hattori's identity for the Hirosaki Museum of Contemporary Art, Tokyo TDC Special Prize 2021: no separate symbol, the identity is the arrangement of the name.

    The mark stands the full height of the type area, and its eight rows become the card's grid. 西頂廣仁宮 spans rows 1 and 2, 西顶文化 sits on row 3, the name's cap top and baseline sit on rows 4 and 5, and the contacts sit on rows 6 to 8. Every line of type sits on a row of dots. To fit, the website is left off the front: the email carries the domain.

    H · 印 Seal, refined

    From Pentagram's identity for the Museum of Chinese in America (Michael Bierut): a square logo combining the name with a modified Chinese chop, restrained, no cliché.

    No frame. The left 36 mm of the card is one field in a VI colour, edge to edge, like a chop's colour on paper: gold by default, or red, teal or mauve grey. On it, centred on one axis, the full dot mark (21 mm) and 西頂廣仁宮. On the charcoal, 西顶文化 holds the top right; the name sits on the top line and the contacts on the bottom line.

    I · 中轴 Axis

    From the temple itself: a Chinese temple compound is laid out on one central axis, its halls in order along it.

    Everything stands on the card's vertical centre line, in the order a formal card gives: the mark, 西頂廣仁宮, 西顶文化, then the person, then two lines of contacts on the bottom line.

    改动Against v1.8's BusinessCard

    Before is the system's own Xiding.card(), drawn live from the v1.8 bundle. After is this card at the same size. The 6 mm margin, the type sizes and the contact block are the system's.

    • Two jobs, two sidesThe back is the brand: the whole dot mark and 西顶学院 · 西顶艺术, nothing else. The front is the person.
    • GroundA teal side and a white side become one charcoal ground, vi-charcoal, the field of the temple's nameplate.
    • BackThe VI's dot mark whole, 16 mm high, and 西顶学院 · 西顶艺术 at 2.8 mm, both in vi-ivory.
    • Dots, frontThe full mark, as rings (the system's Circle style) or as solid dots. Ring strokes at least 0.18 mm so they print.
    • Lettering西頂廣仁宮 in the VI's brush lettering, on the front only, next to the rings, with 西顶文化 directly under it. No English line.
    • PersonName at 6 mm; romanisation, CEO 首席执行官 and the contacts at 2.2 mm on 3.5 mm. The last line always sits 6 mm from the bottom edge.
    • InksCharcoal and ivory. The optional third, vi-sand, marks only the organisations: 西顶学院, 西顶艺术, 西顶文化.
