# Wordmarks

Names set alone, as outlines: no font is needed to show them. Generated from `Xiding.kit()` by `components/src/build-assets.ts`.

- `xiding-wordmark-zh-black.svg` — 西顶, the Chinese wordmark, black, no field. For white and `paper`.
- `xiding-wordmark-zh-teal.svg` — 西顶 in temple teal (#628F8A), no field. For white, at large sizes.
- `xiding-wordmark-zh-white.svg` — 西顶 in white on a temple-teal field.
- `xiding-name-wenhua-black.svg` — 西顶文化 as a brand row, black.
- `xiding-name-yishu-black.svg` — 西顶艺术 as a brand row, black.
- `xiding-name-xinzhi-black.svg` — 西顶新知 as a brand row, black.
- `xiding-name-shiguang-black.svg` — 西顶时光 as a brand row, black.

The names accompany the mark. Lockups with the Latin line XIDING are saved from the AssetKit page, where Instrument Sans is loaded.
