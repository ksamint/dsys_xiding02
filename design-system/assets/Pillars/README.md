# Pillars

The three brands as outlined files, in their fixed inks. Generated from `Xiding.BRAND_DETAILS` by `Xiding.art.pillars()` and `Xiding.art.pillarPlate()`.

- `xiding-pillars-light.svg` — the three brands as three pillars for a light ground: each with its word and name, the base rule, its services under it. Black base and text.
- `xiding-pillars-dark.svg` — the same for a dark ground: white base and text, 西顶新知 in white, 西顶时光 dark with a white edge.
- `xiding-pillar-arts.svg` — 西顶艺术，艺术: a square plate in temple teal (#628F8A), white type.
- `xiding-pillar-academy.svg` — 西顶新知，教育: a square plate in black, white type.
- `xiding-pillar-lifestyle.svg` — 西顶时光，生活方式: a square plate in white with a black edge, black type.

Use the plates as column heads, avatars for the three brands, and tags on printed matter.
