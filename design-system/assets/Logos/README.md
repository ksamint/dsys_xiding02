# Logos

The mark, exported from the mark studio at its measured geometry: a 1124 × 1636 field that already includes the clear space. Show them with `<img>`; the colours are fixed in each file.

- `xiding-mark-teal-dot.svg` — White dots on Temple teal (#628F8A). The first choice.
- `xiding-mark-ink-dot.svg` — Black dots on white.
- `xiding-mark-night-dot.svg` — White dots on black.
- `xiding-mark-ink-ring.svg` — Black circles on white. For large uses.
- `xiding-mark-ink-point.svg` — Black points on white. For large uses.
- `xiding-mark-night-ring.svg` — White circles on black. For large uses.
- `xiding-mark-tealInk-ring.svg` — Teal circles (#628F8A) on white. For large uses.
- `xiding-mark-night-point.svg` — White points on black. For large uses.
- `xiding-mark-ink-dot-transparent.svg` — Black dots, no field. For white and `paper`.
- `xiding-mark-night-dot-transparent.svg` — White dots, no field. For `brand-teal` and black.
- `xiding-mark-tealInk-dot-transparent.svg` — Teal dots (#628F8A), no field. For white.
- `xiding-avatar-teal.svg` — The mark centred on a square temple-teal field, for avatars and app icons.
- `xiding-avatar-teal.png` — The same at 1024 × 1024, for 公众号 and other places that take a picture file.
- `xiding-mark-teal-dot.png` — White dots on Temple teal as a PNG, 1124 × 1636.
- `xiding-mark-points-extracted.json` — The 32 marks and 40 circle centres as measured from the logo image.
- `xiding-mark-points-trued.json` — The same, mirrored about x = 600 and rounded to whole pixels.

Lockups with 西顶 and XIDING are drawn live with `Xiding.mark({ lockup })` or `Xiding.subBrand()`, since their type needs the web fonts. Convert the text to outlines before print.
