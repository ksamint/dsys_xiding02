# Hero

A temple-teal field, the mark standing whole in it, and one line of Songti: the opening of a page, a poster or a deck. Built from `xd-stage`, `Xiding.scatter()` and `xd-vert`.

**You provide** one headline, a kicker (the brand or the programme), and the facts to hang at the right: the tagline, the address or a date.

- The headline is `hero`: Songti bold, 72px, `brand-white` on the field, at its foot. It is a name, a fact, an act or the programme's question (谁能驾驭 AI？), written to the rules in 文案 · Voice.
- The field takes two thirds of the width. The mark stands whole at its right in white; no dot leaves the field.
- The facts are hung vertically on the paper third: the names at 22px in `ink`, the address at 13px in `muted`. Pass their boxes and the headline's as `avoid`.
- One hero per document.
