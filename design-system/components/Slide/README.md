# Slide

A 16:9 slide drawn at 960 × 540 (double every size for 1920 × 1080): one statement, one shape, almost no small text. Classes `xd-slide`, `xd-slide__top`, `xd-slide__title`, `xd-slide__lead`, `xd-slide__take`, `xd-slide__split`, `xd-slide__plate`, `xd-slide__cols`, `xd-slide__num`, `xd-slide__label`, `xd-slide__detail`, `xd-slide__foot`.

**You provide** one statement per slide, and one visual: a brand plate, a set of large figures, a chart or a photograph of the site.

- **Statement slide** — the statement in Songti bold at 80px, on one line, one supporting sentence in `muted` at 24px, and a brand plate on the right in that brand's ink.
- **Figure slide** — the takeaway at 44px, then up to three figures at 150px in `teal-text`, each over a 2px `ink` rule with a label and one line of detail. `teal-text` is the accent: use it for the figures the audience came for.
- The takeaway is a full sentence that states the finding. Every figure carries its source in the footer.
- Top left is the brand row; top right the page number. Bottom right is XIDING ACADEMY in the Latin wordmark style.
- One idea per slide. Lines break at commas, with three characters or more on the last line.
