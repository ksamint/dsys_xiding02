# PillarCard

A square card for a slice title or a quote: the brand's name, its word and one line on that brand's ink, with the brand's signature underneath. Classes `xd-pcard`, `xd-pcard--arts`, `xd-pcard--academy`, `xd-pcard--lifestyle`, `xd-pcard__field`, `xd-pcard__pillar`, `xd-pcard__line`, `xd-pcard__foot`.

**You provide** the brand (`arts`, `academy`, `lifestyle`) and one line, with its line breaks.

- The title formula is the brand's name and one sentence: 西顶时光｜谁在你最难的时候愿意信你. On the card the name and its word (西顶时光, 生活方式) sit at the top and the sentence at the bottom; in plain text the name and the sentence are joined by 「｜」.
- The line is Songti bold at 24px, up to ten characters wide, in two or three lines, broken at a comma or between phrases. The last line holds three characters or more.
- Type on the field is bold and 20px or larger, which keeps white on temple teal legible. The signature sits on `surface`: the mark and the brand's name.
- Use the guest's own words. A guest's name on a card needs their written consent.
- The same card works as a video cover at 16:9 and a subtitle plate: keep the brand ink, the name and one line.

The preview shows each brand's three lines from the public introduction.
