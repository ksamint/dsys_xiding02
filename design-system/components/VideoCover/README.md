# VideoCover

A 16:9 cover for the programme: one title on a pillar's ink, the mark, and a signature strip. Classes `xd-vcover`, `xd-vcover--mei`, `xd-vcover--zhi`, `xd-vcover--li`, `xd-vcover__field`, `xd-vcover__title`, `xd-vcover__sub`, `xd-vcover__mark`, `xd-vcover__foot`.

**You provide** the title, and the pillar when the episode belongs to one.

- **Series title** follows the formula 谁能驾驭 AI？听 XX 讲大美、大智、大礼, on `pillar-zhi`.
- **Episode title** is the guest's name and one concrete choice or decision: XX：我为什么否掉了 AI 给的前三个方案. It sits on the ink of the pillar the episode is about, and the strip names that pillar and its word.
- Titles are Songti bold in one or two lines, broken between phrases, with three characters or more on the last line. Type on the field is bold and 20px or larger.
- The strip is on `surface`: the brand row on the left, the kind of title on the right.
- A guest's name on a cover needs their written consent.
