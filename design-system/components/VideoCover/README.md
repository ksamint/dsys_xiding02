# VideoCover

A 16:9 cover for the programme: one title on a brand's ink, the mark, and a signature strip. Classes `xd-vcover`, `xd-vcover--arts`, `xd-vcover--academy`, `xd-vcover--lifestyle`, `xd-vcover__field`, `xd-vcover__title`, `xd-vcover__sub`, `xd-vcover__mark`, `xd-vcover__foot`.

**You provide** the title, and the brand when the episode belongs to one.

- **Series title** follows the formula 西顶广仁宫 · 听 XX 讲艺术、教育、生活方式, on `ink-academy`.
- **Episode title** is the guest's name and one concrete choice or decision: XX：我为什么否掉了 AI 给的前三个方案. It sits on the ink of the brand the episode is about, and the strip names that brand.
- Titles are Songti bold in one or two lines, broken between phrases, with three characters or more on the last line. Type on the field is bold and 20px or larger.
- The strip is on `surface`: the brand row on the left, the kind of title on the right.
- A guest's name on a cover needs their written consent.
