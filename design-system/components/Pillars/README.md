# Pillars

The three brands as three pillars standing on one base: 西顶艺术, 西顶教育 and 西顶时光. Classes `xd-pillars`, `xd-pillar`, `xd-pillar--arts`, `xd-pillar--academy`, `xd-pillar--lifestyle`, `xd-pillars__base`, `xd-pillars__motto`, `xd-pillars__sentence`; the words come from `Xiding.BRAND_DETAILS`.

**You provide** nothing: the names, the words, the services and the lines are those of the public introduction.

- Order is 西顶艺术, 西顶教育, 西顶时光. Each pillar carries the brand's word in the tagline (艺术, 教育, 生活方式) above its name.
- Each pillar has its brand's ink: `ink-arts` (temple teal), `ink-academy` (black in light, white in dark), `ink-lifestyle` (open, with a 1px `ink` edge). The name is written on every pillar, so the inks tell the brands apart a second time.
- Above each pillar sits its Latin line: XIDING ARTS, XIDING EDUCATION, XIDING LIFESTYLE.
- The baseline is a 2px `ink` rule. Under it each pillar has its three services, then its three lines in `muted`, one to a line.
- Use it once in a document, where the three brands are introduced. The same picture scales to signage and to a slide.
