# SceneMenu

The menu of what happens in the space, one row per brand: the brand on its ink, its scenes joined by the pair dot, and what the scenes are for. Classes `xd-menu`, `xd-menu__row`, `xd-menu__pillar`, `xd-menu__scenes`, `xd-menu__use`; the rows come from `Xiding.SCENES`.

**You provide** nothing for the standard menu. A printed menu adds, per scene, its length, price, host and room.

- Rows follow the IP document: 西顶艺术 养品味, 西顶教育 解决问题, 西顶时光 经营关系, and 担当 养心力 for 心理辅导 and 教练.
- The fourth row is 担当, on a plain `paper` plate (`ink-root`): it is the ground the three brands stand on, and it takes its name from the fourth fixed question (你承担了什么).
- Scene names are Songti semibold at 20px; the purpose sits at the right in `muted`.
- Each scene on the menu answers one question: how it serves a 先行者.
