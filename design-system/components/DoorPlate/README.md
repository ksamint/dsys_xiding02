# DoorPlate

A plate for a room or a scene: its name on the ink of the pillar it serves, with the pillar and its word underneath. Classes `xd-plate`, `xd-plate--mei`, `xd-plate--zhi`, `xd-plate--li`, `xd-plate__field`, `xd-plate__foot`.

**You provide** the scene's name (two to four characters) and its pillar.

- Scenes come from `Xiding.SCENES`: 古琴, 艺术鉴赏 and 策展 serve 大美; 咨询, 培训 and 落地陪跑对接 serve 大智; 生日, 下午茶 and 私宴 serve 大礼.
- The name is Songti bold, set at the bottom left of the field. The strip names the pillar and its word, joined by the pair dot.
- Proportion is 3:4. For print and engraving, keep the three inks as flat fields; the 大礼 plate is white with a 1px black edge.
- Plates carry names. Directions and arrows are still to be designed with the site's floor plan.
