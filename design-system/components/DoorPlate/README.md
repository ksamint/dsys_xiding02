# DoorPlate

A plate for a room or a scene: its name on the ink of the brand it serves, with the brand's name underneath. Classes `xd-plate`, `xd-plate--arts`, `xd-plate--academy`, `xd-plate--lifestyle`, `xd-plate__field`, `xd-plate__foot`.

**You provide** the scene's name (two to four characters) and its brand.

- Scenes come from `Xiding.SCENES`: 古琴, 艺术鉴赏 and 策展 serve 西顶艺术; 咨询, 培训 and 落地陪跑对接 serve 西顶学院; 生日, 下午茶 and 私宴 serve 西顶时光.
- The name is Songti bold, set at the bottom left of the field. The strip names the brand.
- Proportion is 3:4. For print and engraving, keep the three inks as flat fields; the 西顶时光 plate is white with a 1px black edge.
- Plates carry names. Directions and arrows are still to be designed with the site's floor plan.
