# TempleSign

The 西頂廣仁宮 nameplate from the VI's signage page: a 50 cm backlit symbol box, a 150 cm charcoal name panel, drawn to scale (4.3 px per cm).

From the VI's page 2. Sizes: symbol box 50 × 50 cm, 5 cm gap, name panel 150 × 50 cm, 5 cm gap, 5 cm end strip. Craft, as specified: 1 mm galvanised steel plate, laser-cut through for the symbol and the name; milky translucent acrylic behind, with LED light inside; fluorocarbon baked paint; the visitor notes UV-printed in green.

The notes on the panel are the temple's: opening hours 9:00–20:00 (last entry 19:00), closed Tuesdays, 30 visitors a day by appointment, and eleven visitor rules. The card shows the first lines.

Added in v1.8.
