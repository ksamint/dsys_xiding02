# H5Exhibition

An exhibition page: the title on a full-bleed black field with the mark in teal circles, three facts, one button, then the related viewing sessions. Built from `xd-h5--night`, `xd-fieldblock--night`, `xd-rows`, `xd-cta` and `xd-list`.

**You provide** the exhibition's title, its dates, and how to get in.

- Exhibitions are 西顶艺术's, sit under the 时光 tab and take the `ink-arts` dot. The field is flat black with the `ring` mark in temple teal until real photographs of the works and the halls exist; a photograph then replaces the field, with the title under it on `paper`.
- Facts are rows on a 2px `ink` rule: 展期, 地点, 入场. The place is 西顶庙.
- The button is 预约观展. It ends on the pair dot.
- Related 艺术鉴赏 and 策展 sessions follow as list items; the one exhibition-linked session takes the teal action dot.
