**You provide** the brand, the speaker's name and the line being said.

- The plate sits bottom left, `space-24` from both edges, and stays in place for the whole slice.
- The line is the guest's own words, in one line of Songti semibold. Long sentences are split across successive plates.
- The grey field in the preview stands for the footage.
- Guests are filmed in the same room, from the same camera position, in the same light.
